1. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient that is marked "Confidential" at the time of disclosure or, if disclosed orally, is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure and summarized in a writing marked "Confidential" delivered to Recipient within thirty (30) days.

5. Survival. Recipient's obligations survive for five (5) years after termination or expiration.
